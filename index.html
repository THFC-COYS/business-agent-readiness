Business Agent Readiness

Score your business for AI agent adoption in about three minutes. Muse-first by design: the plan you get centers on deploying Muse-powered agents and Meta Business Agent on the channels your customers already use, starting with WhatsApp.

An open toolkit by Greg Lucas, Muse early adopter and advocate. Companion to Muse Exchange and MuSe SIM.

How it works

Fifteen questions across the five readiness dimensions of the AGENT Framework. Answer honestly for where your business is today, not where you want it to be. You get a score per dimension, an overall readiness score, a maturity stage, and a prioritized adoption plan with governance checks and a channel plan.

AcumenKnow where agents create value
GovernanceOwners, approval gates, kill switch
EvaluationMeasure agent work like staff work
NavigationChannels, rollout, and change
TransformationRedesign workflows around agents

The assessment

Scale for every question: Not started (0), Ad hoc (1), In progress (2), Mostly in place (3), In place and measured (4).
